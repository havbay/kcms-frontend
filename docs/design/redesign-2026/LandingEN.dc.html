08 — Why KCMS

Built for Khmer.

Most moderation tools translate a Khmer comment into English and judge the translation. Meaning that only exists in Khmer — who the speaker is addressing, whether a word is an insult or a joke, whether the spelling is a mistake or a way around a filter — is gone before the decision is made.

KCMS reads the comment as it was written.

Khmer script, read directly

Khmerlish and code-switching

Everyday slang and misspellings

Words obfuscated to dodge filters

Criticism of a company, kept visible
