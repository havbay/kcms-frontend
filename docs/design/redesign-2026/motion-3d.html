KCMS redesign · hero motion

Four ways to put the hero in three dimensions.

All four are running below, not described. Each carries what it actually costs: bundle weight, GPU load, and what a phone on a slow connection gets instead.

!

Worth saying before you choose. Three of these need three.js, which is roughly 150 KB gzipped on top of your current bundle — more than React and Motion together. For a trust-and-safety product the risk is not performance, it is tone: a hero that looks like a graphics demo undercuts a page whose argument is “a person decides every action”. The option that costs nothing (3D‑B) is also the one that stays closest to that argument. I would ship 3D‑B, or 3D‑A if you want the concept piece and accept the weight.

Whichever you pick, the rule holds everywhere: under prefers-reduced-motion the scene renders one still frame and stops, and below 768px the WebGL options never load at all — a phone gets the CSS version or a flat image.
