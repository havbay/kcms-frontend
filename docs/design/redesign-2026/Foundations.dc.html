Motion

Eight named motions and nothing else. Each answers a question the reviewer would otherwise have to ask: what arrived, what changed, what did I just do. The demos loop; in the product each plays once.

Reduced motion is a second design, not a downgrade path. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every motion above collapses to an opacity change of 120ms or less, the 3D hero renders one still frame and stops, and the decision collapse becomes an instant removal with the same toast. The reviewer still learns what changed — nothing moves to tell them. Read the setting once with useReducedMotion() and branch the variant rather than disabling animation in CSS afterwards. Below 768px the WebGL hero never loads at all: a phone gets the flat panel on the mobile artboard.
