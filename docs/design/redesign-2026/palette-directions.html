KCMS redesign · step 1 of the rebuild

Six colour directions. Pick one, or mix two.

Each card shows the same two moments: the landing hero, and three queue rows where colour has to carry meaning. Neutrals and typography stay as they are — only the palette changes.

What I read from your references. Neopay: white ground, one energetic brand hue as a soft glow behind the hero and as a filled button, near-black nav pill. The indigo dashboard: white page, one saturated hue owning the app surface.

The catch. KCMS needs three state colours — safe, needs review, harm. If the brand hue is green, "green means safe" stops working. Each option below says how it resolves that.

What is fixed. White ground, cool grey neutrals, Instrument Serif headlines, Manrope interface. Those held up; only the hue is in question.
